### Scott Tse

Senior Product Manager, Data & Interoperability at Reliance eHealth Collaborative, a health information exchange in Oregon. 9 years in healthcare data exchange: HL7v2, C-CDA, FHIR, and the operational reality of moving clinical data between organizations.

**Current project: [Tabula Health](https://github.com/sky-t/tabula-health)**
Describe a patient in plain English, get FHIR R4, C-CDA 2.1, and HL7v2 test records with real terminology codes. The LLM extracts clinical intent; deterministic lookups assign the codes, so nothing is hallucinated. Eval work to date: 55 annotated traces, a 10-code failure taxonomy, and spec fixes for 7 failure modes.
[Live demo](https://v0-tabula-health.vercel.app) · [Failure taxonomy](https://github.com/sky-t/tabula-health/blob/main/docs/FAILURE_TAXONOMY.md)

**Earlier:** civic data science with Hack Oregon's Emergency Response and Disaster Resilience teams (2018 to 2019).
